# Memes (AI/Tech)

## Good Targets
- AI company pricing drama (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google pricing changes)
- Hype vs reality gaps (model launches that underwhelm)
- CEO promises vs actual product
- Dev tools that break or change pricing overnight
- Vibe coding fails
- AI corporate contradictions ("safety first" + "ship fast")

## Formats That Work

| Format | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Screenshot + one-liner | Pricing page screenshot + "Ah yes, affordable." |
| Marketing vs Production | "What they promised" / "What you got" |
| Conversation escalation | Fake Slack: PM asks for "quick AI feature" (3-6 messages) |
| Parody terminal | Error: `wallet_empty. Run: pip install second-mortgage` |
| Before/After | Before AI: 1 IDE. After AI: 7 subscriptions. |
| Fake PR/git blame | `git blame` showing "ai-generated" on every line |

## Roast Calibration

Default Medium. Examples:
- Light: "Oh no, who approved this launch"
- Medium: "Remember when they said it would be free forever? Yeah about that."
- Spicy: Only for genuine bad behavior (fake benchmarks, hidden pricing, etc.)

## Timeliness

- News meme: under 30 min or skip
- Topical: same-day or dead
- Evergreen: can be queued (dev pain, pricing jokes, corporate absurdity)

Emotion → Scene mapping:
- Corporate betrayal: Breaking Bad, Succession
- Pricing shock: The Office, Arrested Development
- Chaos: It's Always Sunny
- Dev despair: Office Space, Wednesday
- CEO delusion: Succession, Silicon Valley
