# LinkedIn Platform

Rules for creating LinkedIn posts. Based on the 360Brew algorithm update (2026) and proven performance data.

---

## Algorithm: 360Brew (2026)

LinkedIn uses an interest-based feed. Key principle: **focused small accounts outperform unfocused large accounts.** Stay in your lane. Topic authority compounds over time.

---

## Ranking Signals (in order of weight)

| Signal | Weight | What it means |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Saves | #1 (5x a like) | People bookmark your post for later |
| Comments from outside network | #2 | New people find and engage with your post |
| Dwell time | #3 | People stop scrolling and read the whole thing |
| Topic authority | #4 | You consistently post about the same topics |
| Shares | Strong | Someone sends your post to their network |
| AI detection | Negative | LinkedIn suppresses unedited AI-sounding content |

---

## Format → Signal Mapping

Use this to pick the right format for your goal:

| Format | Primary Signal | Best For |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Plain text + opinion | Outside-network comments → burst reach | Hot takes, disagreeable opinions |
| Plain text + data/numbers | Saves + dwell time | Stats, pricing, benchmarks, frameworks |
| Meme/image + short text | Shares (strongest single signal) | Humor, reactions, relatable moments |
| Short video (3-10s) | Shares + dwell time | Quick demos, meme clips |
| Arrow list (→) | Saves | Processes, comparisons, step-by-step |
| Carousel (PDF) | Dwell time + saves | Tutorials, frameworks, breakdowns |
| Long text (200+ words) | Dwell time | Stories, in-depth analysis |

---

## Post Formatting Rules

- **Short paragraphs:** 1-3 sentences per paragraph
- **Line breaks between thoughts:** White space = dwell time
- **Hook in first 2 lines:** Everything above the "see more" fold must create curiosity
- **Hashtags:** 3-5, placed at the end (below the fold)
- **Emojis as bullets:** Use sparingly — →, •, ↳ for structure, not decoration
- **Links:** NEVER in the post body (kills reach). Always put links in the first comment.
- **Character limit:** 3,000 characters max, but sweet spot is 800-1,500 for most post types

---

## What Gets Reach

1. **Save-worthy content:** Specific numbers, comparisons, frameworks, actionable advice
2. **Disagreeable opinions:** Takes that invite "I disagree because..." comments
3. **Outside-network comments:** Write things that make people tag their colleagues
4. **Data posts:** Real numbers, pricing, benchmarks — people save these
5. **Timely reactions:** Same-day news with a unique angle

---

## What Kills Reach

- **Unedited AI output** — LinkedIn's AI detection suppresses robotic-sounding content
- **Links in post body** — Put them in comments instead
- **No hook in first 2 lines** — If the start is boring, nobody clicks "see more"
- **Engagement bait** — "Like if you agree" / "Tag someone who..." / polls asking obvious questions
- **Off-topic scattershot** — Posting about 10 different topics destroys topic authority
- **Company shilling** — Promotional content without genuine value
- **Monday posting** — Consistently weakest day on LinkedIn
- **3+ posts per day** — Oversaturation kills individual post reach

## Post Structure Template

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines, above the fold]

[Body — substance, opinion, data]

[CTA — drives comments or shares]

[hashtags]
```

### Hook patterns that work:
- Lead with a number: "$X for Y." / "3 things wrong with Z."
- Lead with disagreement: "Everyone's celebrating X. I think it's a problem."
- Lead with a statement: "[Company] just [did thing]."
- Lead with a question: "Why does [X] when [Y]?"
- Lead with a moment: "I opened my laptop and..."

### CTA patterns that work:
- Invite disagreement: "Am I wrong? Tell me why."
- Ask for experience: "Has this happened to you?"
- Create debate: "What would you do?"
- Ask for additions: "What am I missing?"

## Hashtag Rules

- 3-5 hashtags per post (research-backed sweet spot)
- Place at the end of the post (below fold)
- Lowercase preferred
- Mix broad (#ai, #marketing) with specific (#openai, #contentcreation)
- Don't repeat the exact same set on consecutive posts
- Never generic filler unless directly relevant (#innovation, #futureofwork)

---

## LinkedIn-Specific Anti-Patterns

Beyond the general anti-AI rules, avoid these LinkedIn-specific patterns:
- "I'm excited to announce..."
- "Thrilled to share..."
- "After X years in [industry], here's what I learned..."
- Humble brag format ("People ask me how I [achievement]...")
- Starting with "So..." on every post
- Ending every post with "Agree?" or "Thoughts?"
- Engagement pods (LinkedIn detects and suppresses)
