# Memes Module

Handles humor, roasts, memes, and comedy-driven content.

---

## Core Rule

Comedy is mandatory. Teaching is optional. Funny first, informative second. If it's not funny, it's not a meme post.

---

## What Makes Good Meme Content

**Good targets:**
- Companies making bad decisions
- Hype vs reality gaps
- Marketing vs actual product
- Pricing drama
- Industry contradictions
- Public figures making public claims
- Common pain points everyone relates to

**Never target:**
- Individual people (unless they're public figures making public claims)
- Customers or users
- Anyone based on identity (race, gender, religion, etc.)
- People who can't defend themselves

---

## Meme Formats

| Format | Best For | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Screenshot + one-liner | Pricing changes, announcements, tweets | Screenshot of pricing page + "Ah yes, affordable." |
| Before/After | Industry shifts, tool changes | Before: simple. After: chaos. |
| Conversation escalation | Corporate BS, common pain (3-6 messages) | Fake Slack conversation that escalates |
| Parody terminal/CLI | Bugs, pricing shock, errors | Fake terminal output with absurd error |
| Marketing vs Reality | Hype vs actual product | Split: what they promised / what you got |
| Fake documents | Team dynamics, absurdity | Fake PR review, Slack message, git blame |

---

## Roast Calibration

| Level | Tone | Example | When to Use |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| Light | Playful teasing | "Oh no, who let them ship this" | Default for most things |
| Medium | Pointed criticism with humor | "Remember when they said X? Yeah about that..." | When the company genuinely messed up |
| Spicy | Dark comedy, fake press releases | Parody product recall | Genuine bad behavior only |

**Default: Medium.** Only go spicy when the target genuinely earned it. Never nuclear unless the user specifically asks.

---

## Meme Post Structure

Meme posts are SHORT. The visual carries the content.

```
[Setup — 1-4 lines max]
[Visual/meme]
[Optional one-liner reaction]
```

Do NOT over-explain the joke. Do NOT add a "lesson learned" at the end. The meme IS the post.

---

## Timeliness

- News meme: post within hours or skip
- Topical: same-day or dead
- Evergreen humor: can be queued for any time
- Nothing older than 48 hours unless it's timeless

---

## Conversation Screenshots

For fake Slack/terminal/PR formats:
- Must look authentic
- Use generic names only (Sarah, PM Bot, "senior-dev-42", "marketing-lead")
- Never use real people's names
- Keep to 3-6 messages (short, punchy)
- The punchline is the last message

---

## Video Memes (if video-content module also active)

- Under 10 seconds
- Text overlay must be readable at phone size
- Works without sound
- Scene emotion matches the topic
- Source from: iconic TV scenes, movie moments, reaction clips
